Privacy

When you submit an enquiry, we collect the contact details and training requirements you choose to provide so Results Workplace Training can respond and organise training.

Enquiry details are sent to our team using a third-party email delivery service. Please don't include sensitive personal information in the enquiry form.

To ask about the information you have submitted, please contact Results First Aid using the contact details on their website.

Before launch: This is a provisional privacy notice. The business should review it to reflect its actual handling, retention, disclosure and privacy contact practices.
